# Phase 4 cross-header reconciliation checklist

Scope: every correction that a module's types agent found for a header other than its own
(`out/phase4/*_types_notes.md`), plus the four known items from the task, plus a few conflicts
that a scan of the `// global 0x...` lines turned up (marked "scan"). Nothing in `types/` or
`src/` was changed. This is the list for the fix step.

Status column:
- **verified**: re-checked in this pass against `bin/halo.exe` (objdump or raw .data/.rdata bytes).
- **notes**: the evidence is what the notes cite. It was not re-disassembled here, but nothing contradicts it.
- **reject**: the binary does not support the change. Listed so nobody applies it.

Result column (added after the fix step): **applied**, **skipped** (with the reason) or **open**
(with what is left). Totals: 81 applied, 4 skipped (R82, R83, R84, R85), 0 open (R06, R53 and R79 were closed in the follow-up).

Evidence tools used: `objdump -d -M intel` of the whole `.text`; PE section mapping of
`bin/halo.exe` for .data/.rdata reads; `gcc -fsyntax-only` (host and `-m32`) of every
`out/phase4/*_smoke.c`.
